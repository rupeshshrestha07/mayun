Dear Maya,

Maya, I am really sorry for everything I did. I know what I did was so bad, and I know that saying sorry cannot simply make it all disappear. I know I can't make everything up to you just yet, but I want you to know that I understand the weight of what I did.

I don't expect you to forget it just because I apologized. I don't expect everything to suddenly feel normal again. I know that some things take time, and I know that if I really want to make things right, I have to show you through my actions, not just through words.

I will do everything I can to make things right. I want to learn from my mistakes, become better, and be more careful with your heart. You mean far too much to me for me to take your love, your trust, or your presence for granted.

I love you so much, sanu. You are, and you will always be, my everything. I know I haven't always acted like the person you deserve, and that hurts me to admit. But I want to become that person. I want to be someone who makes you feel safe, loved, respected, understood, and appreciated.

I am really sorry, Maya. Thank you so much for giving me another chance. I don't want to waste it. I will win back your trust, your love, and your confidence in me one step at a time. I know I have to earn those things again, and I am willing to be patient and prove it.

I promise I will do my best to never repeat what I did again. I know a promise means very little without actions behind it, so I want my actions from now on to speak louder than this letter ever could.

There will probably be days when I don't get everything right. I'm still learning, still growing, and still trying to become better. But one thing I don't want to change is how much I love you. I want to keep choosing you, keep caring for you, keep making memories with you, and keep finding little reasons to make you smile.

I love you, and I always will. You mean everything to me, sanu. Thank you for giving your bacha another chance. I am really, truly sorry.

Always your bacha,
Rupesh ♡
